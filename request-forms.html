Request Form Instructions

Please read the following guidelines carefully before filling out this form.

1. How to Fill Out the Form
  • • Complete all required fields accurately.
  • • Make sure your name, address, age, and school information are correct.
  • • Double-check all information before clicking Submit to avoid delays in processing.
2. Required Documents

Before your request can be processed, you must prepare and bring the necessary supporting documents to the barangay office. These may include:

  • • Original and photocopy of your Birth Certificate
  • • Valid ID (Barangay ID, School ID, or any government-issued ID)

*Incomplete documents may delay the release of your requested form.

3. How to Get Your Requested Document

After submitting this online request, wait for confirmation that your document is ready.
Bring all required documents to the barangay office for verification before the clearance or requested form is released.

4. How to Know if It's Ready for Pick-Up

You will be notified once your requested document is ready:
• Through your registered Gmail account, or
• Through your provided cell phone number

Please wait for the official notification before going to the barangay office.
For questions or concerns, you may contact the barangay office directly.
