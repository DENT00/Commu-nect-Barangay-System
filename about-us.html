Our Mission

Service with Integrity and Compassion

We are dedicated to delivering fast, fair, and transparent public services to every constituent of Barangay Gen. T. De Leon — fostering a safe, inclusive, and progressive community through responsive governance and meaningful community engagement.

Our Vision

A Model Barangay for the Modern Era

To be a recognized model of good local governance — a community where every resident has access to quality services, participates in decision-making, and lives in a safe and dignified environment, powered by technology and united by shared values.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Service First

Every decision we make begins with a simple question: how does this serve our residents? Public service is not a job — it is a calling we honour daily.

Transparency

Our constituents have the right to know. We open our processes, our records, and our decisions to public scrutiny without hesitation.

Unity & Inclusion

We serve all residents — regardless of status, origin, or background. Our barangay's strength comes from the diversity of its people.

Sustainability

We build programs and infrastructure with the next generation in mind — protecting our environment and ensuring lasting prosperity.

Innovation

Commu-nect is proof of our commitment to modernizing barangay governance — embracing technology to make services faster and more accessible.

Integrity

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards. Trust is not given — it is earned every day through consistent, honest action.

Our Story

A Brief History

Early 1900s
Founding of the Barangay
Barangay Gen. T. De Leon was established as one of the original barangays of Valenzuela, named in honor of General Timoteo De Leon — a celebrated hero of the Philippine Revolution.
Mid-Century
Community Growth & Industrialization
The barangay experienced significant population growth alongside Metro Manila's expansion. New housing zones, schools, and community centers were established to support its growing residents.
2000s
Modernization of Public Services
The barangay began digitizing records and improving internal processes, laying the groundwork for a more transparent and accessible form of local governance for all constituents.
2026
Launch of Commu-nect
Developed in partnership with Group 10 of Mapua University, Commu-nect launched as the barangay's first official web system — enabling online form requests, account verification, community reporting, and official announcements in one unified platform.
