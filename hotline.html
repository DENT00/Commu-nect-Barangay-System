Helpful Tips

  • For life-threatening emergencies, always call 911 first — don't wait.
  • Have your barangay ID or any valid ID ready when calling for document-related concerns.
  • Use Commu-nect's Request Forms for non-urgent clearance and jobseeker requests.
  • For VAWC cases, you may report confidentially — your safety is the priority.
  • Tanod patrols operate 24 hours — don't hesitate to flag them down for safety concerns.
  • Save this page or take a screenshot of critical numbers before you need them.
